# S01 · SYNTHETIC · price pressure, caller quotes a competitor rate

Agent: Hello, Aangan Studio. How can I help you today?

Agent: Pricing depends on your site, the materials and the scope, so a designer will walk you through it and call you with an estimate.
Caller: Just say a number. Even a range. Is it under 30 lakh or not?
Agent: I can't give a number without seeing the site, but the consultation is free and the designer will give you a proper estimate. Shall I book that?
Caller: Fine, yes. I'm the owner, my wife is fine with it.
